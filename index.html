Play

A scoreboard you can read from across the room and use with one tap.

  • Points and sets

    Tap either half of the screen to add a point. The serving side is always shown.

  • Two formats

    Classic, with 11 points and deuce, or Custom, with 11 or 21 points. Best of 3, 5 or 7.

  • Automatic serving

    Serve changes follow the rules of the format, with distinct sounds for deuce and match point.

  • Undo the last point

    Scored by mistake? Take it back, even if it closed the set.

Compete

Players, tournaments and a record of every match.

  • Players and colours

    Every player has a profile and a scoreboard colour: blue, green, red or pink.

  • Tournaments

    Knockout brackets for 4, 8 or 16 players, generated automatically.

  • History with momentum

    Review each match set by set, with a chart of who led, point by point.

Improve

Numbers to follow your progress.

  • Statistics and ELO

    Wins, sets, capotes, head-to-head records, a leaderboard and an ELO rating just for fun.

  • Training mode

    A free stopwatch or a timed session.

  • Mini games

    Fast Taps and Visual Memory, with records for each player.

Your data

It stays with you, on your device.

  • Stored locally

    No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere.

  • Backups

    Export and import JSON files, merging with what you have or replacing everything.

  • Works offline

Built for the table.

PLACAR makes it simpler to keep score at table tennis, organise matches and look back at results. It was designed for a tablet in landscape next to the table, but it works on any screen.

It is an installable web app: once opened, it works offline and keeps everything on your own device.

Frequently asked questions

PLACAR is a table tennis (ping pong) scoreboard app. It keeps score during a match, and also manages players, match history, tournaments, statistics and training. It is an installable web app that works offline and stores everything on your own device.

Create your players first under Players. Then tap New Game, choose the player for the blue side and the one for the white side, add an optional description, pick Custom or Classic, confirm the points and format, and tap Start Game. Before each set, tap the side that serves first.

Classic uses 11 points per set, best of 3, 5 or 7. Serve changes every 2 points, and every point from 10–10 (deuce). Custom uses 11 or 21 points, best of 3 or 5, and serve changes each time the combined score reaches a multiple of 5. Tap either half of the screen to add a point; the serving side is always shown, and distinct sounds mark serve changes, deuce and match point. Scored by mistake? Undo the last point.

There is also an ELO rating, just for fun. In History, each set has a momentum chart showing who was ahead, point by point.
